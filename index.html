About

A straightforward place to stay in Kajang

FF Relaxhouse is a homestay at Mirai Residence Kajang 2 in Kajang, Selangor, Malaysia. It is set up for short stays and sleeps a maximum of 3 guests.

The focus is a simple, comfortable stay: a queen room, fast Wi-Fi, a TV with streaming, a kitchen you can cook in, and use of the building's shared facilities.

FAQ

Common questions

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM.

What time is check-out?

Check-out is by 11:00 AM.

How many guests can stay?

A maximum of 3 guests. Babies are not counted toward the guest quantity. All staying guests must be declared.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes. Wi-Fi is provided at approximately 600 Mbps–1 Gbps, depending on actual network conditions.

Is parking available?

Parking information is provided to confirmed guests.

Is early check-in available?

Early check-in is possible from 2:00 PM at the earliest, at RM10 per 30 minutes, subject to availability. Please ask the host in advance.

Is late check-out available?

Late check-out is possible up to 1:00 PM, at RM10 per 30 minutes, subject to availability. Please ask the host in advance.

Is a security deposit required?

Yes. The deposit is RM150 for 1 night, RM200 for 2 nights, RM250 for 3 nights and RM300 for 4 nights.

When is the security deposit refunded?

Normally within 12 hours after the regular 11:00 AM check-out time, after the unit and access items have been checked.

Are building facilities available?

Yes. There is a swimming pool at Block A, Level 5 (until approximately 10:00 PM), a gym at Block C, Level 5, and a sauna and jacuzzi (until approximately 12:00 AM). Hours are as currently provided by the host, and building management may change them.
